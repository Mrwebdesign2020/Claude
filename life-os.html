    Questions to decide with

    • How much does it bring in each month that recurs (hosting, maintenance) versus one-off projects?

    • Does it give you energy or drain it after a full shift?

    Operating principles

    • Volume beats intensityDoing the basics every day for months wins over a perfect week followed by nothing.
    • Protect the morning and the eveningThe day job takes 8:30–17:00. The hour before and the two hours after are where you get ahead.
    • Patience on results, urgency on actionsThe 10K, the weight and the next job take months. Today's seven boxes take today.
    • Document, don't performShare what you're actually doing and learning. It builds a name without needing to be an expert.
    • Kill or commitHalf-running a business drains energy. Decide what Mr. Web Design is, then do that fully.
    • Sleep is the multiplierRunning, gym, focus and food choices all get worse on 6 hours. Guard the 22:30 bedtime.
